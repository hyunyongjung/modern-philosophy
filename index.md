A continuing inquiry into things too ordinary to question.

Modern Philosophy begins with ordinary words—oil, water, fire, money, intelligence—and asks what they actually mean.

It is not an attempt to explain established philosophy. It is a record of questions, objections, and revisions through which a personal philosophy gradually takes shape.
